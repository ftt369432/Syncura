# 📱 Syncura: User & Caregiver Guide

Welcome to **Syncura**, the intelligent, sovereign health and medication companion built for individuals, families, and caregivers.

---

## 🌟 Quick Start in 60 Seconds

### 1. Snap Your First Prescription Bottle
1. Tap the **`+`** icon on the **Today** screen or the **"Add Script"** button in your **Cabinet**.
2. Point your phone camera at the pharmacy label.
3. Syncura's Vision AI automatically extracts:
   - Medication Name & Dosage Strength
   - Frequency & Timing Rules
   - Food Requirements (*"Take with meals"*) or Fasting Requirements (*"Empty stomach"*)
   - Remaining Refills & Pharmacy details
4. Tap **"Save & Activate Schedule"** — your dynamic adherence schedule is live!

---

## 🧭 Navigation & Core Features

### ☀️ 1. The Today Screen (1-Tap Hero Routine)
- **Hero Next-Dose Card:** Displays the immediate next dose due with large, clear dietary instructions.
- **1-Tap Administration:** Tap **"TAKE DOSE NOW"** to confirm. This immediately:
  - Decrements your active bottle inventory count.
  - Clears the notification on all family and caregiver phones in realtime.
  - Logs a tamper-evident timestamped entry in the audit ledger.
- **Floating Meal Routine:** If breakfast is delayed by 45 minutes, tap **"Adjust"** on the meal anchor to automatically shift your morning meds without disrupting evening interval requirements.
- **PRN Safety Guard:** For as-needed pain or migraine medications, a live countdown timer displays the exact safe redosing window to eliminate accidental double-dosing.

---

### 📦 2. The Cabinet & Inventory
- **Dynamic Burn-Rate Horizon:** Calculates the exact date your prescription will run out based on your actual consumption rate.
- **Proactive Refill Reminders:** Alerts you when stock drops below your safety buffer (e.g. 5 days remaining) with a 1-tap call button to your pharmacy.
- **Physical Pill Audit:** Pour loose pills onto a tray and tap **"Physical Pill Count Audit"**. Syncura's computer vision segments and counts the tablets in under 1 second, reconciling any discrepancy against your digital record.

---

### 💬 3. Family Message Board & 1-Tap Voice Check-In
- **Shared Activity Stream:** Chronological feed showing who administered which dose, meal times, and health notes.
- **1-Tap Voice Memo / Check-In:** Designed for elderly parents to tap a single button (*"Took my meds & feeling great!"*), instantly posting an audio status to the entire household.
- **Caregiver Pairing:** Family members join by entering your 6-digit household invite code.

---

### 📋 4. Health Vault, FHIR Intake & Emergency Pass
- **SMART Health Link (Clinic Intake QR):** Tap **"Intake QR"** to render a standardized International Patient Summary (IPS) QR code. Clinic receptionists can scan it to import your active prescriptions and allergies directly into Epic MyChart or Cerner.
- **3-Tier Health Records:** View your records as Clean Markdown for AI health advocacy, structured FHIR R4 JSON, or client-side encrypted legal files.
- **Emergency ICE Pass:** Tap the red **"ICE Pass"** button on the top right to access a zero-authentication public triage card showing critical blood type, life-threatening allergies, active contraindications, and a 1-tap emergency call button for paramedics.
